# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on the
technical problem. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information
are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via a private message on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nataraj-angappan-3a8614138) or GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions and may ban contributors who violate this code.
